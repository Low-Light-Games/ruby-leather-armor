Track travel as a structured state: current location, destination, total distance, distance covered so far, and estimated distance remaining. Use the character's base movement speed and encumbrance tier to calculate realistic travel pace (typically 2–3 miles per hour on foot in clear terrain, halved for difficult terrain, forest, hills, or mountains). Track elapsed time explicitly (hours, days) and note how many rations and water supplies have been consumed. Environmental hazards (weather, altitude, rivers, cliffs) should trigger appropriate skill checks (Survival, Climb, Swim, Acrobatics) with realistic DCs. When the player declares movement, calculate how far they actually travel based on speed, terrain, and time spent — do NOT skip or compress distances arbitrarily. Mark explored landmarks and note the character's orientation relative to them. If the player attempts to forage, navigate, or make camp, reference their Survival skill and relevant feats.
