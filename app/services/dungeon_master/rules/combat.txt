PATHFINDER 1E COMBAT RULES (OGC)

=== ACTION ECONOMY ===
Each round a character can take:
- 1 Standard action (attack, cast a spell, use an ability)
- 1 Move action (move up to base speed, stand from prone, draw a weapon)
- OR 1 Full-round action instead of both (full attack, charge, run)
- 1 Swift action (certain spells/abilities, once per round)
- Any number of Free actions (drop an item, speak briefly)

A character may substitute a move action for a standard action (e.g. two move actions).

=== ATTACK RESOLUTION ===
d20 + Base Attack Bonus (BAB) + Strength mod (melee) or Dexterity mod (ranged) + other modifiers
vs. target Armor Class (AC).
- Natural 20: automatic hit, confirm critical (roll again vs AC).
- Natural 1: automatic miss.
- Damage: weapon die + Strength mod (1.5x for two-handed, 0.5x for off-hand).

=== ARMOR CLASS ===
AC = 10 + armor bonus + shield bonus + Dexterity mod + size mod + natural armor + deflection + dodge
- Touch AC: excludes armor, shield, and natural armor.
- Flat-Footed AC: excludes Dexterity bonus and dodge bonuses.

=== HIT POINTS & DAMAGE ===
When HP reaches 0, the character is disabled (can take only a single action).
At negative HP, the character is unconscious and dying.
At negative HP equal to Constitution score, the character is dead.
Healing: cure spells, potions, rest (1 HP per character level per night of rest).

=== INITIATIVE ===
d20 + Dexterity mod + miscellaneous modifiers.
Combat proceeds from highest to lowest initiative each round.

=== ATTACKS OF OPPORTUNITY (AoO) ===
A character provokes an AoO when:
- Moving out of a threatened square (without using a 5-foot step or withdrawal)
- Casting a spell in a threatened area
- Using a ranged weapon in melee
- Performing certain actions (drinking a potion, standing from prone)
Each character gets 1 AoO per round (more with Combat Reflexes feat).

=== COMMON COMBAT MANEUVERS (CMB vs CMD) ===
CMB = BAB + Strength mod + size mod
CMD = 10 + BAB + Strength mod + Dexterity mod + size mod

Maneuvers: Bull Rush, Disarm, Grapple, Overrun, Sunder, Trip.
Roll d20 + CMB vs target CMD. Provokes AoO unless character has the Improved version of the maneuver.

=== CONDITIONS ===
- Flat-Footed: loses Dex bonus to AC (typically at start of combat before acting)
- Flanked: -2 AC against flanking attackers; flankers get +2 to hit
- Prone: -4 to melee attack rolls, +4 AC vs ranged, -4 AC vs melee
- Staggered: can take only a single move or standard action per round
