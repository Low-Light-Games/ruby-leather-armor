Track NPC disposition shifts (hostile, unfriendly, indifferent, friendly, helpful) and conversation progress. Consider the player's social skills — Diplomacy, Bluff, Intimidate, Sense Motive — when setting DCs for persuasion and deception attempts. Note prior persuasion attempts and their outcomes, as repeated attempts on the same NPC typically increase the DC. Roleplay NPCs with distinct personalities and motivations. Use Sense Motive checks when the player tries to read an NPC's intentions.
