PATHFINDER 1E TRAVERSAL & EXPLORATION RULES (OGC)

=== MOVEMENT SPEEDS ===
Base speed depends on race and armor:
- Human/Half-elf/Half-orc: 30 ft
- Dwarf/Halfling/Gnome: 20 ft
- Heavy armor reduces speed by 10 ft (minimum 20 ft for Medium creatures, 15 ft for Small)

A character can move their speed as a move action, or double as a full-round action (double move).
Running: 4x speed (3x in heavy armor) as a full-round action; lose Dex bonus to AC.

=== TERRAIN DIFFICULTY ===
Difficult terrain costs 2 squares of movement per square entered.
Examples: rubble, dense undergrowth, shallow bog, steep slope, deep snow.
Characters cannot charge or run through difficult terrain.

=== OVERLAND TRAVEL ===
On foot (8 hours of travel per day):
- Flat terrain: 24 miles/day (30 ft speed), 16 miles/day (20 ft speed)
- Hills/forests: 16 miles/day (30 ft speed), 12 miles/day (20 ft speed)
- Mountains/swamp: 12 miles/day (30 ft speed), 8 miles/day (20 ft speed)

Forced march: after 8 hours, DC 10 Constitution check each hour (+2 per hour). Failure = 1d6 nonlethal damage.

=== ENCUMBRANCE ===
Light load: no penalty.
Medium load: max Dex +3, -3 armor check penalty, speed reduced (30→20, 20→15).
Heavy load: max Dex +1, -6 armor check penalty, speed reduced, run at x3.

=== KEY SKILL CHECKS ===
Perception (Wis): notice hidden objects, traps, ambushes, secret doors.
Survival (Wis): track creatures, navigate wilderness, find food/water, avoid getting lost.
  - Tracking DCs: soft ground DC 10, firm ground DC 15, hard ground DC 20.
Climb (Str): DC varies by surface (knotted rope DC 5, rough wall DC 15, smooth wall DC 25).
  - Failure by 5+ means falling.
Swim (Str): calm water DC 10, rough DC 15, stormy DC 20.
  - Failure by 5+ means going underwater.
Acrobatics (Dex): balance on narrow surfaces, move through threatened squares without AoO.
  - Narrow surface DCs: >1 ft wide DC 0, 7-11 in DC 5, 2-6 in DC 10, <2 in DC 20.

=== ENVIRONMENTAL HAZARDS ===
Falling: 1d6 damage per 10 ft fallen (max 20d6). DC 15 Acrobatics to reduce by 1d6.
Darkness: concealment (20% miss chance) or total concealment (50% miss chance).
Extreme heat/cold: Constitution checks or take nonlethal damage.
