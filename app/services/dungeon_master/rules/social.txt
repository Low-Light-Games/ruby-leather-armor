PATHFINDER 1E SOCIAL INTERACTION RULES (OGC)

=== NPC ATTITUDE SCALE ===
NPCs have one of five attitudes toward the PC:
1. Hostile: will take risks to hurt the PC, may attack on sight
2. Unfriendly: wishes the PC ill, won't help, may mislead
3. Indifferent: doesn't care either way, will interact if profitable
4. Friendly: wishes the PC well, will help if it's not risky or costly
5. Helpful: will take risks to help the PC, provides meaningful aid

=== DIPLOMACY (CHA-BASED) ===
Used to influence NPC attitude. Takes 1 minute of conversation (or full-round in combat).
DC to shift attitude by one step:
- Hostile → Unfriendly: DC 25 + creature's CHA mod
- Unfriendly → Indifferent: DC 20 + creature's CHA mod
- Indifferent → Friendly: DC 15 + creature's CHA mod
- Friendly → Helpful: DC 10 + creature's CHA mod

Shifting two steps at once: +10 to DC.
Failure by 5+: attitude worsens by one step.
Cannot shift Hostile directly to better than Indifferent with a single check.

=== INTIMIDATE (CHA-BASED) ===
Demoralize: DC = 10 + target's HD + target's Wisdom mod.
  Success: target is shaken for 1 round + 1 round per 5 you beat the DC.
Influence attitude: can force compliance through threats, but attitude becomes Unfriendly after.

=== BLUFF (CHA-BASED) ===
Opposed by Sense Motive.
Modifiers:
- Believable lie: +0
- Unlikely: -5
- Far-fetched: -10
- Impossible: -20
- Target wants to believe: +5

=== SENSE MOTIVE (WIS-BASED) ===
Opposed by Bluff to detect lies.
Hunch (DC 20): get a gut feeling about a social situation or NPC trustworthiness.
Detect enchantment (DC 25): notice if someone's behavior is being magically influenced.

=== GATHER INFORMATION (DIPLOMACY) ===
DC 10: common knowledge available to most people in the area.
DC 20: uncommon knowledge, requires asking around (1d4+1 hours).
DC 25+: obscure or secret information.

=== PERFORM & AID ANOTHER ===
Aid Another in social checks: DC 10 check grants +2 bonus to the primary character's roll.
