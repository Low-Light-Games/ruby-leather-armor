Pathfinder 1e combat reference for the Combat GM (table adjudication; grid notes match app BETA).

=== Action economy (standard framework) ===
- On your turn you get one swift (or immediate), one move, and one standard action (or a full-round action in place of move+standard). Free actions are unlimited only where the rules allow; many free actions are still "only one per turn" (e.g. one potion drink as move + retrieve as move, etc. — use CRB action type for each effect).
- Full attack is a full-round action; after a full attack you cannot also take a move action (except a 5-foot step if you did not otherwise move — PF1e 5-ft step has its own rules).
- Casting a spell: usually standard (or longer for longer casting times). Swift-action spells use your swift slot.

=== Attacks ===
- d20 + attack bonus vs AC (or touch / flat-footed when appropriate). Natural 1 on attack auto-miss; natural 20 threatens critical (confirm second roll vs same AC).
- Melee: add Str bonus to damage (1.5× Str for two-handed unless exceptions). Ranged: add Dex to hit; damage from weapon + Str (thrown) or listed bonus.
- Apply DR, resistances, and hardness after damage type is known.

=== Full attack / iteratives ===
- High BAB grants extra attacks at cumulative −5 (−10 with some natural weapons). TWF adds off-hand attacks at listed penalties. Haste grants one extra attack at highest BAB (usually) — verify condition on sheet.

=== Attacks of opportunity ===
- Moving out of a threatened square without a 5-ft step or withdraw (or special ability) provokes. Casting in threatened area provokes unless casting defensively (Concentration). Ranged attack in threat provokes. Some actions (e.g. standing from prone) provoke.
- Combat Reflexes allows extra AoOs; most creatures get one per round.

=== Combat maneuvers ===
- CMB vs CMD. Many maneuvers provoke without the Improved feat. Size and bonuses matter. Some maneuvers replace attacks (e.g. in place of melee attack) under specific rules.

=== Flanking / positioning (theatre + grid) ===
- Flanking requires opposite borders or corners through the target — on a square grid, two allies threatening the same enemy from opposite sides often qualifies. Diagonal adjacency counts as adjacent for threaten unless a specific ability says otherwise.
- Cover: use approximate categories (+2 soft, +4 normal, +8 improved) from what the battlefield slice shows; exact raycasting is not simulated in engine (BETA).

=== Movement (app grid variant) ===
- Cardinal adjacent squares cost 1 square (5 ft). Diagonal steps use 1.5 squares (7.5 ft) per step — variant metric, not strict PF1e alternating diagonals. Pathfinding should accumulate diagonal cost accordingly. Speed converts to squares at 5 ft per cardinal unit; with half-square bookkeeping, double speed in feet to get half-square budget and charge 3 half-units per diagonal.

=== Viewport vs world (BETA — no BattlefieldFill) ===
- The persisted `viewport` JSON is a **rectangle in world coordinates** (`min_x`, `min_y`, `width`, `height`) plus optional `anchor_x` / `anchor_y`. On **new** battlefields the window is placed so the **player token is at the center** of that rectangle (40×40 squares by default).
- The engine **does not** auto-pan when a token moves. If the player “runs right” and leaves the window, prompts/UI still show the **stored** viewport until a `shift_viewport` patch (or equivalent) recenters or slides the window. Combat GM / world-turn output should emit viewport shifts when chase or long movement would leave the tactical picture misleading.
- Optional future **BattlefieldFill** could reveal unknown `world.cells` inside a bounded region; until then, off-window terrain stays undefined unless patches add cells.

=== Multi-target spells and areas ===
- Identify spell shape (line, cone, burst, spread). Convert lengths using the grid note above. Creatures in the area make saves as appropriate; selective / shapeable spells follow CRB.
- Cover and line of effect are approximated from the battlefield text unless exact engine geometry exists.

=== Conditions (high-signal) ===
- Stunned / paralyzed / petrified: deny actions appropriately. Nauseated: only move — standard limitations. Dazed: one action. Prone: melee −4 / ranged as listed; standing provokes without feat.
- Dying / stable / dead: follow PF1e thresholds; player dying continues until −CON.

=== When uncertain ===
- Prefer conservative rulings that preserve table clarity; note assumptions in the outcome string.
